Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2, 2026

lift. is a workout journal for iPhone made by Ethan Lenz, a developer in California, USA (“we”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app, its widgets and Live Activities, and this website.

The short version

  • Your workouts stay on your device and, if you sign in, in your private iCloud. We can’t see them.
  • Our server only handles your account, invite codes and rest-timer alerts. It never receives your name, your workout history or your Apple Health data.
  • Apple Health data stays on your device and is never used for advertising.
  • We use Meta’s SDK to measure our ads. It gets app opens, purchases and device details, never your workouts or health data.
  • We don’t sell your data, and there are no ads in the app.

Data that stays with you

This information is stored on your device. If you sign in with Apple, most of it also syncs to your private iCloud account, which Apple runs under its own privacy policy. We have no access to any of it.

  • Workouts and history: your workouts, exercises, sets, reps, weights, notes, completed workouts and rest times.
  • Photos and videos you attach to a workout. You choose them with Apple’s photo picker, and lift. only receives the ones you pick.
  • Saved gyms: name, address and map coordinates.
  • Your profile and settings: your profile name, streak and preferences. Your name and earned invite rewards are kept in iCloud so they survive a reinstall.
  • Body weight you enter or read from Apple Health. This stays on the device and doesn’t sync through iCloud.

Apple Health

If you allow it, lift. reads your body weight to estimate the calories you burn, and saves your completed workouts, estimated calories and body weight to Apple Health. We don’t send Apple Health data to our server or any third party, store it in iCloud, or use it for advertising or marketing. You can change access at any time in the Health app.

Location

If you allow it, lift. uses your location while the app is open to find gyms near you. The search goes to Apple Maps. We never receive your location.

Siri, Spotlight and on-device AI

lift. adds your workouts and records to your device’s search index so Siri and Spotlight can find them, and can use Apple’s on-device model for spoken training summaries. None of this comes to us. Anything Siri does with it is handled by Apple.

Reminders and purchases

Workout and streak reminders are scheduled on your device. Apple handles all payments, and we never see your card or billing details.

Data on our server

lift.’s server runs on Supabase in the United States. This is everything it stores:

Your account

If you sign in with Apple, a random account ID and the ID Apple assigns you for lift. We don’t ask Apple for your email address, and your name is never sent to the server.

Kept until you delete your account.

Sign-in sessions

The IP address and device type of your session, and the security tokens that keep you signed in.

Kept until you sign out or delete your account.

Invites

Your invite code, which account used a code and when, and whether the reward was credited. People who invite you and people you invite only see that someone joined, not who they are.

Kept until that account is deleted. If you delete yours, friends who used your code keep a record that they already redeemed one, without your code, so each account can only redeem once.

Rest alerts

When a rest timer starts, the app sends a push token for its Live Activity, when your rest ends, and what the Live Activity shows: your current exercise, sets completed and when the workout started. This lets it alert you even when lift. isn’t open. Apple Push Notification service delivers the alert.

Deleted as soon as the alert goes out or is cancelled, at most 2 hours later.

Anonymous accounts

If you use rest alerts without signing in, the app creates an anonymous account with a random ID that isn’t tied to your Apple Account.

Deleted after 60 days. The app creates a new one if you’re still using rest alerts.

Supabase also keeps request logs, including IP addresses, for a limited time to run and secure the service. Deleted data can remain in logs and backups for a short time.

Meta (Facebook) ad measurement

lift. includes Meta’s SDK so we can tell whether our ads on Facebook and Instagram lead to installs and purchases. When you open the app or buy something, it sends Meta:

  • The event: that the app was opened, or what was bought with its price and currency.
  • Device details: model, iOS version, language, time zone, carrier, screen size, processor count and app version.
  • An ID the SDK creates for this install, and your IP address.

We’ve turned off collection of Apple’s advertising identifier, and lift. doesn’t ask for permission to track you across other companies’ apps. Meta doesn’t receive your name, workouts or health data. Meta handles what it receives under its own privacy policy.

Email and this website

If you email us, we use your address and message to reply, and keep them as long as they’re useful for support. Our support email runs on Gmail. This website has no cookies, analytics or scripts. It’s hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visitors’ IP addresses.

Why we use your data

  • To run the features you use (account, invites, rest alerts). Legal basis in the EEA and UK: performing our contract with you.
  • To keep the service secure and stop invite abuse. Legal basis: our legitimate interests.
  • To measure whether our ads work. Legal basis: our legitimate interests.
  • Apple Health, location, photos and notifications only work with your permission, which you can withdraw in iOS Settings or the Health app.

We don’t sell your data, show ads in the app, share data with data brokers, or use Apple Health data for advertising or marketing.

Who receives data

  • Apple: iCloud, Apple Health, Apple Maps, push notifications, Sign in with Apple, Siri and payments.
  • Supabase: hosts our server in the United States. Its data processing terms include the EU’s Standard Contractual Clauses for data transferred from the EEA, UK and Switzerland.
  • Meta: ad measurement, as described above.
  • Google: only if you email us, through Gmail.

We only use these companies for the purposes above, and we rely on their terms to protect your data at least as well as this policy does. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to a new owner if lift. is ever sold, in which case this policy keeps applying until you’re told otherwise.

Deleting your data

  • Your account: in lift., go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. Apple asks you to confirm, then this deletes your server account, invite code and pending alerts, your profile, and your synced data in iCloud, and ends lift.’s Sign in with Apple access. If you’re offline, the server part finishes the next time you open the app. Workouts saved on the device and rewards you’ve already earned are kept.
  • Your workouts: go to Settings → Data → Delete All Data to remove your workouts, history, gyms and attached media. Deleting the app removes what’s stored on that device.
  • Sign in with Apple: to stop using it with lift., go to iOS Settings → your name → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple.
  • Permissions: turn off Apple Health access in the Health app, and location, photos and notifications in iOS Settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including the EEA, UK, Switzerland, California and other US states, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal data, to object to or limit how it’s used, and to withdraw consent. We won’t treat you differently for using these rights.

To make a request, email liftyiosapp@gmail.com. Because the server doesn’t hold your email address, we may ask for your invite code to find your records. If you’re in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

lift. is for people 13 and older. It isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their personal data. If you think a child under 13 has given us data, email us and we’ll delete it.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections, your session is stored in the iOS Keychain, and the database only lets each account reach its own records. No system is perfectly secure, so we can’t guarantee nothing will ever go wrong.

Do Not Track

lift. and this website don’t respond to browser Do Not Track signals.

Changes

When we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we’ll also tell you in the app before it takes effect.
