Common Questions

How do I manage or cancel my lift+ subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Open Settings on your iPhone → tap your name → Subscriptions → lift. You can also manage billing from lift. under Settings → Billing.

Is my workout data backed up?

Your data lives on your device. If you create a profile with Sign in with Apple, your workouts, records, and stats sync securely to your private iCloud.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the lift+ paywall in the app and tap “Restore.” Make sure you’re signed in to the same Apple Account used for the original purchase.

How do I delete my data or account?

In lift., go to Settings → Data to clear history or delete all data, and Settings → Account → Delete Account to remove your profile and synced iCloud data.
